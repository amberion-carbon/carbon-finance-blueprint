1. Current State Analysis

After analyzing all three workbooks (2024, 2025, 2026), here is how Carbon currently manages finance operations. The system lives entirely in Excel spreadsheets, with one workbook per year.

Workbook Structure

Monthly Sheets (Jan-Dec)
The core calculation engine. Each month has its own sheet containing every active client, their assigned contractors, rate configurations, hours/days worked, payment calculations, and invoice tracking. This is where the real work happens.
Contractors Sheet
Master registry of all contractors with their legal entity name, VAT ID, bank account details, and a monthly grid tracking: total paid, invoice number, and payment status (paid/unpaid) per month.
Clients Sheet
Monthly invoice totals per client, with sent date, due date, and payment status. Tracks AR at the aggregate level per client per month.
Totals for Model
Summary dashboard showing Carbon Margin and Client Total per month per client. Used for high-level financial reporting. Many cells show #REF! errors due to cross-sheet formula breaks.
Contractor Hours
Monthly hours per contractor grouped by client. Used to cross-reference time tracking with the billing calculations in the monthly sheets.

2. How Billing Currently Works

Carbon operates as a staffing intermediary. Contractors have a base rate, Carbon adds a fee, and clients are billed the combined total. The monthly sheets calculate everything.

The Rate Calculation Flow

Contractor RateHourly, Daily, or Monthly
→
Carbon FeePer unit markup
→
Client RateRate + Fee = Total
→
Time WorkedHours or Days
→
Invoice TotalsContractor + Client

Three Rate Models

ModelContractor GetsCarbon FeeClient PaysExample
Hourly Hourly Rate × Hours Fee × Hours Total Rate × Hours Cristian Slabu: €62.50/hr + €100/hr Carbon Fee = €600/day. 160 hrs → Contractor: €10,000. Client: €12,000.
Daily Daily Rate × Days Fee × Days Total Rate × Days Constantin Dogaru: €600/day + €150/day fee = €750/day. 22 days → Contractor: €13,200. Client: €16,500.
Monthly Flat monthly amount Flat monthly fee Flat total Abdalla Nayer: $4,000/mo + $1,000 fee = $5,000/mo. No time tracking needed.

Carbon Fee Structures Observed

The Carbon Fee varies significantly by client and contractor. Some patterns from the data:

  • Percentage-based: Many contractors show a consistent % markup. Hidden Road contractors have ~20-25% margins. Marley Spoon varies from 16-22%.
  • Flat fee per unit: Some contractors have a fixed fee per hour/day (e.g. Piotr Rogowski: €87.78/day Carbon Fee on a €399/day rate = ~22%).
  • Monthly flat fee: For monthly-rate contractors like Abdalla Nayer, Carbon adds a fixed $1,000/month regardless of workload.
  • Variable by client: The same contractor (Anamaria Burduaza) has different Carbon Fees on different clients: €8/hr on Marley Spoon vs €6/hr on Apriority.

AP and AR Tracking

Accounts Payable (Contractor Side)
Per contractor per month: Contractor Total amount, Invoice Number (from the contractor), Received status (boolean: did they send their invoice?), Paid status (boolean: did Carbon pay them?). Tracked in both the monthly sheets and the master Contractors sheet.
Accounts Receivable (Client Side)
Per client per month: Invoice Total (sum of all contractor totals + Carbon fees), Sent Date (when Carbon sent the invoice), Due Date (payment deadline), Payment Status (Sent/Paid/Unpaid). Tracked in both the monthly sheets and the master Clients sheet.

3. Pain Points & Why This Needs to Be Software

Broken Formulas Everywhere
The 2026 Totals for Model sheet is riddled with #REF! errors. Cross-sheet references break when rows are added or clients are reorganized. The summary dashboard is unreliable.
Manual Duplication
The same data lives in multiple places: monthly sheets, Contractors sheet, Clients sheet, Contractor Hours sheet. Every month, someone updates all four, hoping nothing falls out of sync.
No Automation
Invoice cross-checking, payment status updates, month-end rollups: everything is manual. There is no connection to banking, email, or project management tools.
Staffing Only
The current structure only handles time-based staffing (hourly/daily/monthly contractors). Product studio, recruitment, and BOT projects have fundamentally different billing models that don't fit the spreadsheet layout.
No Audit Trail
Rate changes, corrections, and adjustments leave no history. If a number is wrong, there's no way to trace what changed and when.
Year Boundary Problem
Starting a new year means creating an entirely new workbook. Historical reporting across years requires opening multiple files and cross-referencing manually.

4. Platform Vision: Carbon Finance Hub

A custom web application purpose-built for Carbon's operations. Not just an invoicing tool: a finance operations hub that handles all service types and integrates with the tools Carbon already uses.

Service Types It Must Support

Staffing
Time-based contractor placements (hourly, daily, monthly). Multiple contractors per client. Carbon fee/markup per contractor. Exactly what the current spreadsheets handle, but automated.
Product Studio
Project-based billing with milestones, deliverables, and potentially mixed models (fixed-fee phases + time-and-materials). May involve internal Carbon team + contractors working together.
Recruitment
One-time or retainer-based fees. Success fees as a percentage of annual salary. Milestone payments (shortlist, interview, placement). Guarantee period tracking.
BOT (Build-Operate-Transfer)
Phased engagement: build a team, operate it, then transfer to the client. Monthly operational fees during operate phase, with planned ramp-down. Transfer milestones and exit payments.

Core Billing Models the Platform Needs

ModelUse CaseBilling Logic
Time & MaterialsStaffing, Product StudioRate × Time + Carbon margin
Fixed MonthlyStaffing, BOT operate phaseFlat fee per month per contractor or team
Milestone-BasedProduct Studio, RecruitmentFixed amounts tied to deliverables/events
Percentage FeeRecruitment% of placement salary or project value
RetainerProduct Studio, RecruitmentFixed monthly access fee, with or without rollover
HybridAny complex engagementRetainer + T&M overages, or milestone + time tracking

5. Data Model

The database schema needs to be flexible enough to support all service types while keeping the familiar concepts from the current spreadsheets.

Core Entities
// Organizations & People Client { id, name, legal_entity, billing_contact, payment_terms (net_15, net_30, net_45), currency, tax_id, address, status } Contractor { id, name, legal_entity, vat_id, bank_account, bank_name, payment_method (direct, Deel, Xolo), currency, country, status }
Engagements & Assignments
// An engagement is a project/contract with a client Engagement { id, client_id, name, type (staffing | product_studio | recruitment | bot), billing_model (time_materials | fixed_monthly | milestone | percentage | retainer | hybrid), start_date, end_date, status, notes } // Links a contractor to an engagement with their rate config Assignment { id, engagement_id, contractor_id, rate_type (hourly | daily | monthly), contractor_rate, carbon_fee, client_rate, currency, start_date, end_date, status } // Track rate changes over time (audit trail) RateHistory { id, assignment_id, contractor_rate, carbon_fee, client_rate, effective_date, changed_by, reason }
Time Tracking & Billing
// Monthly time entries per assignment TimeEntry { id, assignment_id, period (YYYY-MM), hours, days, source (manual | imported | api), notes, submitted_at, approved_at } // Milestones for project-based billing Milestone { id, engagement_id, name, description, amount, currency, due_date, completed_date, status } // The billing period calculation result BillingPeriod { id, engagement_id, period (YYYY-MM), line_items: [{ assignment_id, contractor_total, carbon_fee, client_total }], total_contractor, total_carbon_fee, total_client, status (draft | approved | invoiced) }
Invoices & Payments
// AR: Invoices sent to clients ClientInvoice { id, client_id, billing_period_id, invoice_number, amount, currency, issued_date, due_date, status (draft | sent | paid | overdue | void), paid_date, paid_amount, pdf_url, sent_via } // AP: Invoices received from contractors ContractorInvoice { id, contractor_id, assignment_id, period, invoice_number, amount, currency, received_date, due_date, status (expected | received | verified | paid), paid_date, paid_amount, file_url, verified_against_billing_period } // Cross-check: does the contractor invoice match what we calculated? InvoiceVerification { id, contractor_invoice_id, billing_period_id, expected_amount, invoiced_amount, discrepancy, status (match | mismatch | resolved), resolution_notes } // Batch payment groups (per client per period) PaymentBatch { id, client_id, period (YYYY-MM), status (draft | approved | processing | completed | partial_fail), total_amount, currency, approved_by, approved_at, executed_at } BatchPayment { id, batch_id, contractor_invoice_id, contractor_id, amount, currency, provider (revolut | wise | deel), provider_txn_id, status (pending | sent | settled | failed), sent_at, settled_at, failure_reason }

6. Feature Breakdown

Dashboard & Reporting

The home screen replaces the broken "Totals for Model" sheet. Real-time, always accurate.

  • Monthly P&L overview: Total revenue, total contractor costs, Carbon margin, broken down by client and service type.
  • AR aging: Which client invoices are outstanding, approaching due date, or overdue. Color-coded status.
  • AP status: Which contractor invoices are expected, received, verified, or paid this period.
  • Cash flow forecast: Based on payment terms and historical patterns, project expected inflows and outflows.
  • Margin analysis: Margin % by client, by contractor, by engagement type. Trend over time.
  • Year-over-year comparison: No more opening three separate workbooks.
Engagement & Assignment Management
  • Create engagements with flexible billing models. A single client can have multiple active engagements (staffing + product studio, for example).
  • Assign contractors to engagements with per-assignment rate configurations. The system calculates the client rate automatically.
  • Support for contractors working across multiple clients (like the spreadsheets already handle).
  • Rate change management with effective dates and full audit history.
  • Engagement status lifecycle: Active, Paused, Completed, Cancelled.
Monthly Billing Cycle Engine

This replaces the monthly sheet creation workflow. The system guides you through each period:

1. Input TimeEnter or import hours/days
→
2. Auto-CalculateSystem computes all totals
→
3. ReviewCheck calculations, flag anomalies
→
4. Generate InvoicesClient invoices created
→
5. Track PaymentsAP and AR status
  • Smart defaults: For monthly-rate contractors, the system pre-fills amounts. For hourly/daily, it prompts for time entries.
  • Anomaly detection: Flag if hours are unusually high/low compared to previous months, or if a contractor hasn't submitted time.
  • Batch operations: Mark multiple contractor invoices as paid, generate all client invoices for the month in one click.
Invoice Management
  • Client invoice generation: Auto-generate PDF invoices from billing period data. Customizable templates per client.
  • Contractor invoice intake: Upload contractor invoices (PDF/image). OCR parsing to extract invoice number, amount, date.
  • Cross-check engine: Automatically compare contractor invoiced amounts against calculated amounts. Flag discrepancies for review.
  • Status tracking: Full lifecycle from draft → sent → acknowledged → paid, with date stamps at each stage.
  • Reminders: Automated reminders for overdue client payments and missing contractor invoices.
Contractor Registry

Centralized contractor profiles replacing the master "Contractors" sheet.

  • Full contractor profile: legal entity, VAT ID, bank details, payment method (direct, Deel, Xolo), preferred currency, country.
  • Assignment history: which clients, what rates, what periods.
  • Invoice history: all invoices received, payment dates, running totals.
  • Document storage: contracts, NDAs, onboarding docs linked to the profile.
  • Availability and status: Active, On bench, Offboarded.
Multi-Currency Support

The current spreadsheets use both EUR and USD. Some clients are billed in USD while their contractors are paid in EUR (or vice versa). The platform needs to handle currency at every level: client billing currency, contractor payment currency, and automatic conversion for margin calculations.

Batch Payment Processing (Revolut / Wise)

Once contractor invoices are verified against confirmed time entries, the platform generates batch payment files. No more paying contractors one by one.

The Verification-to-Payment Flow

1. Time ConfirmedHours/days approved for the period
→
2. Invoice ReceivedContractor submits their invoice
→
3. Cross-CheckSystem matches invoice vs. calculation
→
4. Batch CreatedApproved payments grouped by client
→
5. Execute PaymentSend via Revolut or Wise API

Batch Grouping by Client

Payments are grouped per client engagement. For example, the "Hidden Road" batch for January would contain 7 contractor payments totaling $77,532 in one approval step. This mirrors how the spreadsheet currently groups contractors under each client heading, but adds the automation layer.

Payment Queue
All verified contractor invoices land in a queue. Finance reviews the batch, adjusts if needed, and approves with one click.
Smart Routing
Routes each payment to the correct provider based on the contractor's profile: Revolut for EU/IBAN, Wise for international (Brazil, Serbia, UAE), Deel for platform-managed contractors.
Pre-Payment Validation
Before executing, the system checks: bank details on file, amount matches verified invoice, no duplicate payment for the same period, sufficient balance in the source account.
Confirmation & Reconciliation
Records Revolut/Wise transaction IDs, marks invoices as paid with timestamps, sends Slack notifications. Daily sync with bank transaction history to confirm settlements and flag failed transfers.

7. Integration Architecture

The platform should be the single source of truth, with n8n as the automation backbone connecting everything.

Carbon Finance Hub
Web app with API. Central database for all finance data.
n8n
Automation engine. Orchestrates workflows between all systems.
Notion
Project tracking, client wikis, contractor profiles sync.
Slack
Notifications: overdue invoices, new time entries, approvals needed.
Google Workspace
Gmail for invoice sending. Drive for document storage. Sheets for exports.
Invoice Parser
OCR/AI parsing of incoming contractor PDFs. Extract amounts, dates, numbers.
Revolut Business
Batch SEPA payments to EU contractors. Balance checks. Transaction reconciliation.
Wise Business
International batch payments (Brazil, Serbia, UAE). Multi-currency FX. Recipient management.
Deel
Sync payment status for Deel-managed contractors. Invoice matching.

Key n8n Workflows

Monthly Billing Cycle Automation
  • Trigger: 1st of each month (or manual trigger)
  • Pull time data from Notion or other tracking tools into the platform
  • Auto-calculate billing for all active engagements
  • Send Slack notification to finance team: "January billing ready for review"
  • After approval, generate client invoices as PDFs
  • Send invoices via Gmail with tracking
  • Create follow-up reminders in Google Calendar
Contractor Invoice Processing
  • Trigger: Contractor email with invoice attachment arrives in a designated Gmail inbox
  • n8n extracts the PDF attachment
  • Sends to invoice parser (OCR + AI extraction)
  • Matches against expected billing in the platform
  • If match: auto-mark as verified, notify via Slack
  • If mismatch: flag for review, notify finance team with details
Payment Status Sync
  • Trigger: Daily check or bank webhook
  • Check bank account for incoming payments matching outstanding client invoices
  • Auto-reconcile payments against invoices
  • Update invoice status in the platform
  • Post summary to Slack: "Hidden Road paid January invoice ($95,375.90)"
  • Update Notion project page with payment status
Overdue Invoice Alerts
  • Trigger: Daily at 9am
  • Query platform for invoices past due date
  • Send Slack alert to finance channel with aging summary
  • Auto-send gentle reminder email to client after 7 days overdue
  • Escalate to a dedicated Slack thread after 14 days overdue
Batch Payment Execution
  • Trigger: Finance team approves a batch in the platform
  • n8n receives webhook with batch details (contractor IDs, amounts, currencies, bank details)
  • Routes each payment to the correct provider: Revolut for EU/SEPA, Wise for international, Deel for Deel-managed contractors
  • Calls Revolut Business API or Wise API to create the batch transfer
  • Monitors payment status via polling or webhook callbacks
  • On success: updates platform with transaction IDs and timestamps, marks invoices as paid
  • On failure: flags the specific payment, notifies finance via Slack with error details
  • Posts batch summary to Slack: "February Marley Spoon batch: 6 payments sent via Revolut ($28,940), 1 via Wise ($4,000). 1 Deel sync pending."
Notion Sync
  • Sync engagement status and key metrics to a Notion dashboard
  • When a new client is created in Notion, auto-create in the finance platform
  • When a contractor assignment changes in the platform, update the Notion project page
  • Monthly summary pages auto-generated in Notion with financial highlights

9. Implementation Roadmap

Phase 1 — Weeks 1-4
Foundation: Replace the Spreadsheets

Database setup, contractor/client management, assignment configuration with the three rate models. Manual time entry. Auto-calculation of billing periods. Basic dashboard showing what the "Totals for Model" sheet was supposed to show. Import existing data from the 2026 spreadsheet.

Phase 2 — Weeks 5-8
Invoice Lifecycle

Client invoice PDF generation. Contractor invoice upload and parsing. Cross-check engine to verify contractor invoices against calculated amounts. Full AR and AP status tracking with date stamps. Basic payment recording.

Phase 3 — Weeks 9-12
Automation Layer

n8n integration: monthly billing workflows, Slack notifications, Gmail invoice sending. Contractor invoice email ingestion and auto-parsing. Overdue payment alerts. Notion sync for project/engagement status.

Phase 4 — Weeks 13-16
Multi-Service Expansion

Add Product Studio, Recruitment, and BOT billing models. Milestone-based billing. Retainer management. Engagement type-specific dashboards and reporting.

Phase 5 — Weeks 17-20
Advanced Features

Cash flow forecasting. Multi-currency with exchange rate management. Bank reconciliation automation. Advanced reporting: margin trends, client profitability, contractor utilization. Data migration from 2024 and 2025 workbooks for historical reporting.
